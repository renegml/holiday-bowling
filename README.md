# Holiday Bowling

Head-steered adventure bowling. A decorated bowling ball rolls itself through Candy Country, a candy tour of the pen
pals' winter holidays in the USA and Sweden (Unit 4, Holiday Pen Pals). Turn your head to nudge the ball into the pins
along the way; a full rack of ten waits at the Holiday Fair at the end. Made for students who steer with their
head (webcam face tracking, all on your own computer), a mouse or the arrow keys.

Open index.html from a web server (it needs https or localhost for the webcam). Everything is in this folder.

Built with three.js (MIT), cannon-es (MIT) and MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache 2.0, in ./mediapipe). The sounds in
./sfx are CC0 recordings from Freesound (see sfx/CREDITS.txt).
